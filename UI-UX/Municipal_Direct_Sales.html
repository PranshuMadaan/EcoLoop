Tier 2 — Pickers without a Phone

Same routes. Official rates. Zero middlemen.

Offline rag pickers keep collecting along their normal routes — but at the end of the day they sell directly at the municipal counter using a physical QR card. The counter pays the official rate-card price instantly in cash or UPI, and EcoLoop records exactly how much extra the picker earned versus a middleman sale.
