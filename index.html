THE ECOMIRAI ARCHITECTURE

What is EcoLoop & Why Does It Matter?

Indian urban local bodies (ULBs) face a severe structural breakdown: waste pickers are underpaid in cash, municipal collection tippers run suboptimal routes leading to fuel wastage, and dry waste collection centres (DWCCs) are cut off from direct industrial recycling demand.

1. Spatial Logistics Heuristics

By modeling Ward 14 as a metric distance graph, Google OR-Tools solves the daily CVRP for electric tippers, cutting diesel burn by 31.2% and reducing municipal transport costs from ₹850/MT to ₹600/MT.

Explore Spatial Engine →

2. B2B Scrap Disintermediation

DWCC collection centres auction compressed bales directly to verified recyclers (e.g., Chitose GreenTech) via real-time WebSocket feeds, minting statutory CPCB Plastic EPR tokens with cryptographic SHA-256 hashes.

Explore B2B Trading Desk →

3. Informal Dignity & UPI Rails

Waste pickers ("Green Technicians") like Ramesh Kale (#SW-342) audit household segregation with camera QR scans and get paid instantly via Bluetooth scale telemetry and direct NPCI UPI disbursements.
